What is in the set

Six prompts, in the order I use them. The first one sets your context. The other five push back.

  1. // 01
    Give it your real context firstFor the start of any chat about your career or a call you care about.
  2. // 02
    Five angles on one decisionFor a decision you are close to making and want challenged.
  3. // 03
    It already failed. Tell me why.For a decision you have made, or are about to, before you go further.
  4. // 04
    Argue for the option I am about to rejectFor two real options, when you are fairly sure which one you want.
  5. // 05
    Find what I am taking for grantedFor any plan that feels solid.
  6. // 06
    Catch it agreeing with youFor the end of any long chat, especially one where you like the answer.
