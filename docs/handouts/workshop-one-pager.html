AI without getting burned

I build AI tools for a living and my own business runs on the ones I build. In ninety minutes, in a small room with your laptop open, you build three of them for yourself and take them home running. They keep working after you walk out, and they keep working with the wifi off.

Fig. 01 What you walk out with

What you build

01

A business brain

One file that holds what you sell, who buys it, and what you charge. Paste it in once and every answer after that comes back about your business instead of a business that does not exist.

02

A decision council

You hand it a decision. Three advisors argue it out in front of you, and then one of them calls it.

03

A simplicity check

Point it at a plan you are about to run and it strips out the parts that only look clever.

Fig. 02 How the room runs

What the session is like

Bring a laptopEverything gets built on it, by you, while I am standing there. You leave with the three tools running on your own machine.
It stays with youNothing you type leaves your laptop. You can prove that yourself by pulling the wifi down in the middle of the session and carrying straight on.
Small groupKept small on purpose, so your own questions get answered out loud instead of saved for the end.
A working sessionThis is not a talk. If what you want is somebody to build it for you and hand it over finished, that is a different conversation and I am glad to have it.

Fig. 03 Who runs it

Boubacar

I run Catalyst Works. I sit with owner operators who still answer their own phone, most of them under twenty people. We work out where the week is getting stuck and what is worth pointing at it first.

The tools in this session are the ones I built for myself first. I teach them because they earned their place in my own week, not because they demo well in a room.
