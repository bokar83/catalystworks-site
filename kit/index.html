Three steps, and it stops living in one project

        
Make it follow you everywhere

        
The council you just built lives inside one project. You have to go find it. A tool you have to go find is a tool you stop using by Tuesday. Three steps and it comes with you instead, into every conversation, on every device, whether you ever open that project again or not.

      

    


    

      
Step 1 Ask Claude to package it

      
Open a new chat inside the project you built tonight and paste this. Claude writes the file. If a download appears, take it. If one does not, take the ready made copy below. Either one works, so do not wait on it.

      


Take the Decision Council instructions we used in this project and package them as a skill I can use outside this project.

Make a folder called decision-council with one file in it called SKILL.md.

At the top of SKILL.md put exactly this:
---
name: decision-council
description: Runs a decision through three passes. Five advisors argue it from five directions, then they peer review each other, then a chairman finds where they agree, names where they split, and delivers one verdict of proceed, change or kill. Use when the user asks for a decision council or brings a business decision they are weighing.
---

Under that, put the full Decision Council instructions, unchanged.

Then zip the decision-council folder and give me the zip to download.

        
      

      

      

        
If no download appeared, or it will not open, take mine. Same steps from here, nobody left behind.

Step 2 Upload it to your account

      
This is the part that makes it yours for good. Four steps and a file picker.

      
    
        
  1. Settings, then Capabilities. Check that Code execution and file creation is switched on. Most accounts have it on already. Switch it on if yours does not.
    2. 
        
  2. Customize, then Skills. This is a different menu from Settings. This is where people get lost, so look for the word Customize.
    3. 
        
  3. The plus button, then Create skill, then Upload a skill. Pick the zip you just downloaded.
    4. 
        
  4. Switch it on. The toggle sits with it in your skills list.
    5. 
      

      
On a phone right now? Do this one on a laptop at home. The upload lives on the desktop site. And if the menu names moved since tonight, look for Skills. That is the one that matters.

    


    

      
Step 3 Prove it followed you

      
Open a brand new chat. Not the project, and nothing in it. Then ask it for the real decision you gave the council earlier, in one line, off the top of your head. Your own, not an example. If you cannot think of one, the line below works.

      


Run my decision council on this: whether to take on the customer who always pays thirty days late.

        
      

      
It works because the council is part of your account now, not part of one conversation.

    


    

      
Then do the other two. Same three steps for the Business Brain and the Simplicity Check, with their own name and description in place of the council's. Do them before Monday buries it.

The rule that keeps you out of trouble

      
The AI drafts. A human approves. Nothing carrying your name goes out without you reading it first. Not a quote, not a contract, not an email to a customer. That is the brake pedal, and it is the whole safety plan.
