What you build

  1. A business brain One file that holds what you sell, who buys it, and what you charge. Paste it in once and every answer after that comes back about your business instead of a business that does not exist.
  2. A decision council You hand it a decision. Three advisors argue it out in front of you, and then one of them calls it.
  3. A simplicity check Point it at a plan you are about to run and it strips out the parts that only look clever.

Then a set of recipes to take home, and a cheat sheet built to print.

What the evening is like

  • Bring a laptop. Everything gets built on it, by you, while I am standing there.
  • Nothing you type leaves your machine. You can prove that yourself by pulling the wifi down in the middle of the session and carrying straight on, which is the demonstration I would rather give you than a promise.
  • Small group on purpose. Your own questions get answered out loud.
  • Sessions run in person. If you are too far out to sit in the room, email me and I will tell you what I can do from here.

This is a working session and not a talk. Come with a laptop and ninety minutes, and you leave with the three tools running.

If what you want is somebody to build it for you and hand it over finished, that is a different conversation and I am glad to have it. Email me and we will start there instead.
