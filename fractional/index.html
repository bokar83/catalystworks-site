One seat, whatever the job turns out to be

Same job. Different name depending on what your company needs this year.

Some companies need someone to lead AI. Some need someone to lead people. Most companies your size need one person who can do both and does not need a title to do it. I take that seat one day a week and I take whatever the job actually is.

Why these are one job

It is the same question asked twice. What in this business should stop being done by hand, and who owns it after that.

AI answers the first half. People answer the second. A company that automates something and never decides who owns it has not fixed anything, it has moved the mess somewhere quieter. And a company that reorganizes its people around a process nobody has looked at in four years has just made the old process faster.

That is why I do not carry these as two services. It is one job with two names, and which name is on the door depends on what your company is short of this year.

Who this is for

Owners of businesses with a team the work runs through, roughly ten to fifty people, with no operations, IT or HR function to hand any of this to. If you are a one person shop, this is the wrong page.

The job is to make the job go away

Most retainers are built to grow. This one is built to shrink.

I am not trying to become the person you cannot operate without. That is a bad deal for you and a fragile business for me. Your people end up running this, and my time on your calendar goes down every quarter until you drop a tier, hand it to somebody permanent, or tell me we are done.

Months 1 to 3 The heaviest stretch. We pick the thing costing you the most, build it or fix it, and write down how it works so it does not live in my head.
Months 4 to 6 Your people run it. I review, unblock, sit in on the hard ones, and fix what breaks. You should feel me getting quieter.
Month 7 onward You step down a tier, hand it to a permanent hire, or we stop. I will tell you when I think it is time rather than wait for you to ask.

Why this is not just a smaller salary

Two people in these seats full time is two fixed costs. Salary each, then benefits and payroll taxes on top, commonly put at somewhere near 1.4 times the salary once you add everything up. Then the months before either of them knows your business. Then the severance conversations if it does not work. Then two headcount approvals before any of it can start.

This is a variable cost, and it is one person instead of two. You turn it up during a build, down once it runs, and off when you are done. That is the trade, and it is a different shape of cost rather than a discount on the same one.

The first 30 days

  • Week 1. I sit with you and the handful of people the work actually runs through. I am hunting the one thing costing you the most, on either side, not building a list of everything that is wrong.
  • Week 2. You get it in writing. What is slowing you down, what it costs in hours and dollars a month, and which one we fix first. If the document says you do not need me, that is what it will say, and you keep the document either way.
  • Weeks 3 and 4. We start fixing. Something real is running before the first month is out, with the person who owns it already trained on it.

Do not hire me if

  • You want a plan handed to you so you can run it yourself. I stay. Staying is the product.
  • You are a one person shop. There has to be a team for the work to run through.
  • You want AI on it because somebody on your board asked about AI. I will tell you no, and you will have paid me to say it.
  • You want any of this kept away from your people. I train them. That is not a bonus, it is the deliverable.
  • You need payroll and benefits administration, or a pair of hands on a ticket queue. Both are the wrong thing to buy from a person.

What I am not going to show you

A wall of client logos. I am early in this on my own, and I would rather you hear that from me than work it out on your own.

What I can show you is the thing I run my own business on. A system I built myself that finds my leads, drafts my work, and tells me every morning what to do first. Not a demo. The actual thing, live, on the call, with the rough edges still in it.

I have already crossed the bridge everyone is still talking about. That is the proof I have today. If it is not enough, I would rather you pass now than be disappointed in month three.

How many of these I can hold

A handful at a time. That is arithmetic and not a sales line: at a day a week each, I run out of week. When I am full I will tell you I am full.

Tell me what is slowing you down

Twenty minutes, no deck. You describe what is stuck, I tell you whether this is a fit and roughly which tier it would be. If I am not the right person for it, I will say so on the call.

I use your email to follow up about this and nothing else. No list, no sequence you did not ask for.
