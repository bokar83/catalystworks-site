Fractional AI leadership

I help companies put AI to work without the confusion and the overwhelm.

Most owners feel like they have to use AI right now. I help you take a breath and get clear on whether you even need it, where it fits, and how to make it work. When advice is not enough, I take the seat and run it myself.

What you are actually buying

A day of my week, inside your business.

MonTueWedThuFri

Not a deck, and not a strategy phase that ends with a PDF and an invoice. I pick the thing that is costing you the most, build what fixes it, and sit next to the person who has to run it after I leave the room.

Some weeks that means choosing a tool and wiring it into how you already work. Some weeks it means telling you to leave AI out of it, because a checklist and one changed handoff would do the same job for free. You will never pay me to bolt AI onto a problem that did not need it.

Who this is for

Owners of businesses with a team the work runs through, roughly ten to fifty people, with no operations or IT function to hand this to. If you are a one person shop, this is the wrong page. There has to be a team for the work to run through.

The job is to make the job go away

Most retainers are built to grow. This one is built to shrink.

I am not trying to become the person you cannot operate without. That is a bad deal for you and a fragile business for me. Your people end up running this, and my time on your calendar goes down every quarter until you drop a tier, hand it to somebody permanent, or tell me we are done.

Months 1 to 3 The heaviest stretch. We pick one thing, build it, and write down how it works so it does not live in my head.
Months 4 to 6 Your people run it. I review, unblock, and fix what breaks. You should feel me getting quieter.
Month 7 onward You step down a tier, hand it to a permanent hire, or we stop. I will tell you when I think it is time, and I would rather say it early than bill you for a month you did not need.

Hours run high while we are building and taper once the thing runs. That is why the tiers step down instead of the invoice staying flat forever.

Why this is not just a smaller salary

A person in this seat full time is a fixed cost. Salary, then benefits and payroll taxes on top of it, which is commonly put at somewhere near 1.4 times the salary once you add everything up. Then the months before they are useful. Then the severance conversation if it does not work. Then the headcount you have to justify before any of it can start.

This is a variable cost. You turn it up during a build, down once it runs, and off when you are done. No headcount to approve, no ramp, no benefits, no severance. That is the trade, and it is a different shape of cost rather than a discount on the same one.

The first 30 days

You should be able to tell whether this was worth it before the second invoice.

  • Week 1. I sit with you and the two or three people the work actually runs through. I am hunting the one thing costing you the most, not building a list of everything that is wrong.
  • Week 2. You get it in writing. What is slowing you down, what it costs in hours and dollars a month, and which one we fix first. If the document says you do not need me, that is what it will say, and you keep the document either way.
  • Weeks 3 and 4. We start fixing. Something real is running before the first month is out. Not a roadmap for later. A thing that works, with the person who owns it already trained on it.

Do not hire me if

  • You want a plan handed to you so you can run it yourself. I stay. Staying is the product, and if you only need the plan you should buy the audit instead.
  • You are a one person shop. There has to be a team for the work to run through or there is nothing here to fix.
  • You want AI on it because somebody on your board asked about AI. I will tell you no, and you will have paid me to say it.
  • You want to keep this away from your people. I train them. That is not a bonus, it is the deliverable.
  • You need a pair of hands on a ticket queue. This is judgment plus building, and it is the wrong way to buy staffing.

What I am not going to show you

A wall of client logos. I am early in this, and I would rather you hear that from me than work it out on your own.

What I can show you is the thing I run my own business on. A system I built myself that finds my leads, drafts my work, and tells me every morning what to do first. Not a demo and not a screenshot. The actual thing, live, on the call, with the rough edges still in it.

I have already crossed the bridge everyone is still talking about. That is the proof I have today. If it is not enough for you, I would rather you pass now than be disappointed in month three.

How many of these I can hold
